These govern what is TRUE. They do not say what anyone KNOWS.

- Royal protocol and chain-of-command in 1950s Britain may be dramatized but should remain plausible; staff speak and act within plausible constraints of loyalty and discretion.
- Public reactions are constrained by 1950s press practices: newsreels, print newspapers, wire services and gossip columns, not instant global dissemination.
