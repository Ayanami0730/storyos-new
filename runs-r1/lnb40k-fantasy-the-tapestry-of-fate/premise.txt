Write a complete fantasy novel of about 40,000 words.

Premise:
Amina al-Sirafi, a pirate, is coerced by an immortal council of peri to sail to an island inhabited by witches from which no one can escape and to steal a single spindle that has the power to rewrite fate.

Central conflict:
Under threat from an immortal peri council, Amina must infiltrate an inescapable island of witches and steal a fate-rewriting spindle despite the island's supernatural dangers.

Characters:
- Amina al-Sirafi — pirate (protagonist)
- Peri Council — immortal peri who coerce the mission
- Witches of the Island — inhabitants/guardians of the island
- The Spindle — the artifact that can rewrite fate (objective)

The finished novel must contain all of the following:
- The protagonist is the pirate Amina al-Sirafi.
- An immortal peri council compels or coerces Amina to undertake the mission.
- The mission destination is an island inhabited by witches from which no one can escape.
- Amina's explicit objective is to steal a spindle capable of rewriting fate.

Write the novel itself — no outline, no commentary, no notes to the reader.
