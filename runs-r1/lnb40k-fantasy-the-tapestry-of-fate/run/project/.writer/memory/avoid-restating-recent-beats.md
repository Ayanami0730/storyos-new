When fixing a repeated-beat finding, cut the repeated dialogue and replace it with a short reference that the beat occurred earlier, then use that space to dramatize new, scene-unique beats (e.g., a map touch, a small mishap, a concrete action). Habit: I tend to restage a debate instead of advancing. Remember to look for the previous scene's line and compress it here.
