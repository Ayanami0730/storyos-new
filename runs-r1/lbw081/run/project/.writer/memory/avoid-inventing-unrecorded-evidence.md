When a detail is missing from the index (e.g., an unread monogram, an untested ash brand), do not present it in prose as a fact. Either: (1) mark it as a deliberate, auditable invention in the state delta with a clear supersede, or (2) keep the prose acknowledging the gap and record admissions or inferences separately. Basis: I asserted a monogram that the packet listed as missing and had to revert it.
