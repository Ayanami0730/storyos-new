---
title: promise-placement
hook: Pay promises where you promise them.
scope: project-convention
source: s-004
last_verified_at: 2026-07-27T15:28:28.669Z
---
When a scene establishes a promise with a due-by marker, either resolve it in that scene or explicitly defer it there and in the plan. Leaving the promise unpaid until the final scene reads like a dropped promise even if it is later resolved; the fix is to either (a) move payoff earlier (touches earlier scenes and risks contradicting later material), or (b) mark the promise as intentionally deferred in the originating scene and the plan. For this project choose (b) unless the payoff must precede other developments.
