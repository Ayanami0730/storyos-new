### world-rules  <world/rules.yaml>

These govern what is TRUE. They do not say what anyone KNOWS.
A character knows a rule only where canon says so; otherwise they may be ignorant of it, wrong about it, or in the middle of working it out — and if the story is about someone discovering one of these, writing it into their thoughts early is how that story gets destroyed.

- Imagination transforms the children's perception but does not alter physical reality: no magical changes happen to objects or people.
- Objects cannot be created or teleported; anything used in a later scene must be introduced here in the plan as an entity.
- Characters' ages, relationships, and personalities remain consistent across scenes.
- Consequences of physical actions are realistic (traffic is dangerous, bridges under repair are impassable without detours).
- Dialogue and events should respect the tone established here: gently adventurous, grounded, and suitable for a middle-school audience.
