Write a complete fantasy novel of about 20,000 words.

Premise:
In wartime Hong Kong, inside the cramped alleys and stacked rooms of Kowloon Walled City, Mercy Chan makes a living as a spirit‑driven exorcist called in to unravel disturbances no ordinary explanation can hold. When a new case brings her into contact with a particularly malignant revenant, she discovers this presence is not only a furious ghost but also a living wound in the neighborhood’s memory—its power fed by the violence and loss tied to the city’s recent past. Mercy must navigate the maze of the Walled City, negotiate with frightened residents and worn traditions, and perform rituals that test the boundary between supernatural horror and the unresolved historical traumas that seem to animate it. The narrative should be developed as a long-form fantasy that entwines spectral threats with the social and emotional aftermath of war, allowing space for deep character arcs, layered mystery, and the slow revelation of how ghost and history feed each other.

Central conflict:
Mercy Chan must confront a uniquely vicious vengeful spirit whose malevolence is amplified by the wartime traumas of Kowloon Walled City, forcing her to battle both a supernatural antagonist and the community’s buried wounds.

Characters:
- Mercy Chan — the exorcist protagonist, experienced in rituals and negotiation with restless spirits.
- The Vengeful Spirit — an especially malevolent ghost whose animus is bound up with wartime suffering.
- Kowloon Residents — neighbors and witnesses who experience the haunting and carry collective memories of the war.
- An Elder Storyteller — a keeper of local oral histories and ritual knowledge called on for guidance.
- A Young Refugee — a displaced wartime survivor whose personal loss intersects with the spirit’s influence.

The finished novel must contain all of the following:
- Set the story in World War II–era Hong Kong with primary scenes located in Kowloon Walled City, conveying its dense, maze‑like urban environment and the wartime atmosphere.
- Make Mercy Chan the central protagonist: an exorcist/ghost‑hunter who is called to investigate and combat the haunting.
- Portray the antagonist as a particularly malicious vengeful spirit whose power is intertwined with historical trauma from the war—so that haunting operates on both spectral and sociohistorical levels.
- Include detailed exorcism attempts and ritual practices that have consequences for both individuals and the wider community.
- Develop the tale at novel length (approx. 60,000 words) to allow sustained exploration of character development, community dynamics, and the slow unspooling of the ghost’s origin tied to wartime events.

Write the novel itself — no outline, no commentary, no notes to the reader.
