Write a complete fantasy novel of about 20,000 words.

Premise:
A supposedly benevolent institution—known to outsiders as the Mercy Academy—accepts one hundred girls each year into its care, yet after ten years only three ever walk back out; among those who entered by choice is Rue, who sold herself into the Academy as a young woman and spends the rest of her life there, until in her old age a war finally arrives at the Academy's gates and forces the past and the place to reckon with one another.

Central conflict:
Rue must confront the long-term consequences of having bound herself to an institution that keeps almost everyone it takes, as an encroaching war threatens the Academy and forces hidden realities into the open.

Characters:
- Rue — the woman who willingly entered the Mercy Academy by selling herself.
- The Hundred — the yearly cohort of girls purchased or acquired by the Academy (collective).
- The Three — the rare survivors who leave the Academy after ten years (collective).
- Headmistress — the senior administrator and public face of the Academy.
- War Envoy — a representative or manifestation of the war that reaches the Academy in Rue's old age.

The finished novel must contain all of the following:
- The story must feature an institution that annually acquires one hundred girls.
- It must establish that after a ten-year period, only three individuals ever leave the institution.
- The protagonist, Rue, must have entered the institution by selling herself into it.
- A war must arrive at the institution during Rue's later years, affecting her and the Academy.

Write the novel itself — no outline, no commentary, no notes to the reader.
