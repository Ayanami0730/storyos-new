- char-rue: Rue: the protagonist. Sold herself into the Mercy Academy as a young woman. She spends decades inside and ages there; resolute, observant, versed in the Academy's rituals and archives.
- group-the-hundred: The Hundred: the yearly cohort of girls the Academy takes into its care. An institutional number, a social body with shifting members and shared customs.

- char-war-envoy: War Envoy: an emissary and later manifestation of the approaching war — part diplomat, part herald of force — who arrives at the Academy's gates during Rue's later years.
- loc-mercy-academy: Mercy Academy: the institution itself — a walled compound of dormitories, classrooms, a ceremony hall, gardens, an archive and a sealed ledger room. Known outwardly as a benevolent house of care.
- loc-ceremony-hall: Ceremony Hall: the Academy's public-facing space where intakes, pledge-signings, and public audiences with the Headmistress occur.
- loc-dormitory: Dormitory: where the Hundred sleep and keep their limited possessions; a place of whispered alliances, small rebellions and nightly reckonings.
- loc-archive: Archive: the Academy's records room, holding intake folios, contracts, and the private ledger that traces each girl's status and fate.
- loc-garden: Garden: a carefully tended courtyard inside Academy walls. The place where girls are allowed to gather, work, and speak more freely — and where Rue does much of her thinking.
- loc-gates: Gates: the Academy's outer gate and the road beyond. The point at which the war and its emissaries first make contact with the Academy.
- obj-contract: Contract: the binding document girls sign (or the Academy signs for them) on intake. It defines the ten-year term and the Academy's claims; for Rue it is the instrument she once sold herself to.
- obj-folio: Folio: the intake ledger used in Ceremony Hall. It records names, provenance, dates, and occasionally marginal notes that tell of exceptions — where The Three are noted, or where a girl vanishes.
- obj-bell: Bell: a bronze bell in the courtyard rung at set times — intake, curfew, alarms. Its single clear note marks the Academy's rhythms and the day the war arrives.

### world-rules  <world/rules.yaml>

These govern what is TRUE. They do not say what anyone KNOWS.
A character knows a rule only where canon says so; otherwise they may be ignorant of it, wrong about it, or in the middle of working it out — and if the story is about someone discovering one of these, writing it into their thoughts early is how that story gets destroyed.

- Every year, the Mercy Academy acquires exactly one hundred girls; that number is an institutional constant and must appear in the story's facts.
- After a ten-year period within the Academy, only three individuals ever leave under normal circumstances; this must be stated and preserved as a factual statistic within the narrative.
- Rue entered the Mercy Academy by selling herself; her consent and the Contract she signed must be an established fact in her backstory.
- A war arrives and reaches the Academy's gates during Rue's later years; the war must affect events at the Academy and force reckonings with its past.
- The Folio and the Contract are authoritative objects inside the Academy: they record intake and the terms of binding. Any scene that refers to who is allowed to leave must trace to these artifacts or to a documented exception.
- Do not contradict the above institutional facts anywhere in the story; if new facts about the Academy's practices are introduced, they must be consistent with the above constraints or be revealed as covert exceptions with provenance in the Archive.
