### world-rules  <world/rules.yaml>

These govern what is TRUE. They do not say what anyone KNOWS.
A character knows a rule only where canon says so; otherwise they may be ignorant of it, wrong about it, or in the middle of working it out — and if the story is about someone discovering one of these, writing it into their thoughts early is how that story gets destroyed.

- No supernatural explanations: every effect must have a physical, human-scale cause.
- All mechanical trickery and forensics must be physically plausible with late-19th/early-20th-century to contemporary hand tools and clockwork; no exotic or high-tech gadgets.
- Locked-room means the study's door is locked from the inside and windows are latched; there are no unknown or unexplored secret passages unless explicitly named as an entity in the plan.
- Detective's methods are limited to human observation, conversation, reconstruction, and standard local forensic input (char-dr-simms); no omniscient leaps or unavailable lab results.
- Every clue introduced in a scene must have a discoverable physical source (an object, a mark, a document, or a witness) present in the plan's entity list.
