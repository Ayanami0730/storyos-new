When building an attic scene packet, always check: presence/size/orientation of windows; where light falls; whether attic has its own door/lock type; whether trunks are locked or brittle; padding/labels/notes inside trunks; whether objects are catalogued in family papers; smell profile (oilcloth, tar, wood dust); and details about mirror anomalies (dustless surface, warp, time-layer behaviour). This prevents the writer from inventing provenance or physical details without provenance.
